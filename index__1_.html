About us

About us

Zaina Medical is an assistant that helps people understand what's happening with their health before they see a doctor, in the language they're most comfortable in.

Every answer is grounded in real medical content, not invented. Questions are matched against a library of medical articles from Altibbi, one of the largest Arabic health platforms, using semantic search so responses stay close to trustworthy source material.

It's built to inform, not replace medical care. Think of it as a starting point for understanding symptoms, lab results, and general health questions — always confirm anything important with a licensed doctor.

FAQ

What can I ask the assistant?

Ask about symptoms, general health topics, medication information, or attach a lab result and get help understanding it in plain language.

What can't it do?

It can't diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or handle emergencies. For urgent symptoms, contact your local emergency number immediately.

Where do the answers come from?

Responses are grounded in Altibbi's medical articles, retrieved for each question using semantic search, so answers stay close to real, reviewed medical content.

Is my conversation private?

Each chat is tied to a temporary session, not a permanent account. If you request a summary, it's emailed to your sign-in address.

Which languages are supported?

You can chat or talk in English or Arabic — switch anytime from the call screen or the language toggle.

Can I upload a lab result?

Yes — attach an image or PDF up to 8MB and the assistant will walk you through it.
